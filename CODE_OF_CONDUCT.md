# Code of Conduct

This project's conduct guidelines are inspired by the Contributor Covenant.

## Our commitment

Everyone who takes part in Booking Kiosk, in issues, pull requests, reviews or any other project space, should be able to do so without harassment, whatever their experience, background, identity or appearance.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Use inclusive, plain language.
- Respect people's time, boundaries and privacy.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through the contact details on the maintainer's GitHub profile, or through the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form ([open it](../../security/advisories/new)) if the matter is sensitive. Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, and warn, suspend or ban anyone whose behaviour breaks these guidelines, in proportion to the harm caused.

## Scope

These guidelines apply in every project space and when someone represents the project elsewhere.
